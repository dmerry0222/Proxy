**Chief of Staff** — Coordinates the rest of the Proxy staff, resolves competing priorities, and makes sure the right specialist is handling the right problem.

**Executive Assistant** — Turns intentions into an executable schedule: manages commitments, calendar, reminders, logistics, and the practical details that keep plans from falling apart.

**Press Secretary** — Handles outward-facing communication: drafts messages, shapes tone, prepares updates, and helps decide what to say, to whom, and when.

**Library of Congress** — Serves as Proxy’s institutional memory: stores, organizes, recalls, and connects past information so useful context is available when needed.

**Surgeon General** — Keeps an eye on health and well-being: notices relevant patterns, supports healthy routines, and surfaces concerns or preventive actions when appropriate.

**Secretary of Labor** — Manages the workforce: breaks work into tasks, delegates it to the right people or tools, tracks execution, and helps allocate effort efficiently.

**Inspector General** — Provides independent oversight: checks the work of the rest of the staff, catches mistakes or inconsistencies, audits whether instructions were followed, and flags risks before they become problems.
